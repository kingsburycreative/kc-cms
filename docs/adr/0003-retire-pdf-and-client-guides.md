# 0003: PDF guides and per-client guide pages retire into /help

Date: 2026-11-17. Decided by: Rob (help section replaces the PDF guide, 2026-11-16; one guide plus modules, 2026-11-17).

## Context

BeSciWorks has its own guide at /guides/besciworks (11 sections). About eight of them are the same for every client; Papers and the post types are BeSciWorks' own.

## Decision

- The core guide is built by splitting the shared sections out of the BeSciWorks guide; its Papers, Weekly, What Works and Insight Briefs steps become module pages.
- Once /help is live and approved, /guides/besciworks redirects to /help (permanent redirect), and no new PDF guides are made.

## Consequences

- Existing BeSciWorks links keep working.
- The BeSciWorks guide stops being edited the day /help goes live.
